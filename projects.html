Oasis - Color Events

Oasis is a powerful and versatile mobile application that serves as an event diary. This application is designed to help you easily manage and keep track of all your events and tasks. It provides a variety of features and functionalities that make it easy for you to organize your schedule and stay on top of your tasks.

One of the main functions of Oasis is the ability to create events with descriptions, priority levels, locations, avatars, and times. This makes it easy for you to organize your events and ensure that you don't forget anything important. You can also set alarms to remind you of upcoming events, so you never miss anything.

Another key feature of Oasis is the ability to customize the color themes. This means that you can choose a color that suits your personality and preferences. You can also create charts to visualize your schedule and see how much time you spend on different tasks.

The application also provides a variety of different charts and analytics to help you better understand your event patterns and behaviors. You can use this information to optimize your schedule and ensure that you are spending your time efficiently.

Overall, Oasis is a powerful and versatile event diary application that can help you stay organized and on top of your tasks. Its many features and functionalities make it easy to manage your schedule and ensure that you never miss anything important. With its customizable color themes and powerful analytics, it is the perfect tool for anyone who wants to take control of their schedule and maximize their productivity.

More features:

Oasis can generate statistics and charts based on the events you have added over time. Use these insights to explore your event history and see information derived from your recorded events.

Notification priority also affects how precisely notifications are scheduled. Lower-priority notifications can help reduce battery usage when exact timing is less important.

Choose the appearance that works best for you with multiple light and dark themes.

Easily search through your events and notes to find the information you need. Events can also include locations, making it easier to keep relevant information together.

Notifications can be customized with colors and sorted to help you quickly identify and organize your upcoming events.

Set notifications for your events and choose how important they are:

  • Important - for events that require more precise and reliable notifications.
  • Regular - a balanced option for everyday events.
  • Unimportant - suitable for less important events and designed to reduce battery usage.
